Scansor

Explore fitting CAD surfaces to a scan, in your browser.

Select points on a simplified nozzle, fit geometric features, and build a recipe you can edit and replay. When CAD is enabled, try surface construction and STEP downloads in this bounded experiment.

Open the browser experiment

A small example, with clear limits

The experiment opens the packaged nozzle. General scan uploads and full-resolution scans are future work. CAD operations have limits; fitting this example does not guarantee a complete closed solid. Tested in desktop Chromium on Linux; other browsers and mobile remain unverified. Initial loading and CAD can take time and use substantial memory.

Computation stays on your device

The site downloads the application, example and runtime files. Selection, fitting and CAD computation run in a browser worker on your device, without a Python computation server. The experiment has no analytics or scan-upload service; the hosting provider still receives ordinary requests for the site files.

Keep your recipe

Download your recipe before reloading or leaving. You can upload it to replay committed actions on the same example. Draft edits and computed results are not saved automatically. Cancel requests a safe stop; Force and restart is a separate choice when computation does not respond.
