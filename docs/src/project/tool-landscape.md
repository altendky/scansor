# Tool Landscape

## Status

**Research finding, snapshot dated 2026-07-24.** This is a concise orientation,
not an exhaustive market report or a verified feature matrix. Product editions,
licensing, access, and capabilities can change and require direct evaluation.

## Gap Conclusion

No publicly verified turnkey product has been found that satisfies the complete
Scansor contract: user-declared topology and correspondence; fixed, free, and
derived bounded parameters; hard, soft, and diagnostic relationships; joint
robust optimization; identifiability and coverage diagnostics; auditable
parameters; and constrained CAD publication.

This conclusion identifies an unverified product gap, not proof that no private,
specialized, or newly released solution exists.

## Adjacent Tools

The following tools overlap portions of the workflow and should be evaluated
against specific experiments rather than broad assumptions:

| Tool | Adjacent role | Interaction/access posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SpatialAnalyzer Relationship Fitting | Metrology-oriented relationship fitting | Commercial expert tool; likely closest conceptual neighbor, but the complete contract is not publicly verified |
| QUICKSURFACE Pro | Scan-to-CAD and parametric reverse-engineering workflows | Commercial, primarily visual workflow |
| PolyWorks | Inspection, measurement, and geometric fitting | Commercial metrology suite; detailed fit semantics require evaluation |
| Geomagic Design X | Scan-to-CAD reverse engineering | Commercial, primarily visual and feature-oriented workflow |
| Verisurf | Metrology, inspection, and reverse engineering | Commercial expert suite; complete contract not verified |
| ZEISS INSPECT | Inspection and geometric analysis | Commercial metrology workflow; complete contract not verified |
| Grasshopper with Galapagos | Visual model authoring plus optimization | Visual authoring is flexible, but a dependable fitting product would require substantial custom construction |
| Mesh2Surface | Reverse-engineering workflows | Commercial visual tooling; exact joint constrained fitting behavior requires evaluation |
| Creaform software | Scanning and reverse-engineering ecosystem | Commercial hardware/software ecosystem; specific product capabilities require evaluation |
| CloudCompare | Point-cloud inspection, segmentation, and processing | Accessible desktop tool with visual and command-line capabilities; promising as an observation adapter |

The practical distinction is not simply feature count. Some options offer
integrated "just use it" expert workflows, some emphasize visual authoring, and
some can be extended through scripting or custom construction. Access and cost
vary substantially, and no price claims are recorded here without a maintained
source and edition context.
